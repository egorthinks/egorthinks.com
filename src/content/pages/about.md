## About this site

I'm Egor Fedorov, a founder building tools for developers who code with AI and quietly suspect it's eroding their skills.

This is where I write about attention, memory, dopamine, and what happens to your brain after eight hours of accepting Claude's diffs.

No course. No bootcamp. No "level up your career" pitches. Just notes from someone trying to keep his mental models intact while using the same tools that make them slip.

If anything here resonates, the best place to find more is my [writing](/blog) or [X](https://x.com/egorthinks). For one-to-one, email me at `egor.fedorov.contact@gmail.com`.
